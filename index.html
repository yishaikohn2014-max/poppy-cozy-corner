A note about talking to Poppy

Your browser handles voice listening and may send audio to its speech provider. Poppy's spoken replies use a voice available in your browser, which may use its own provider too. This app does not save or send audio or messages to its own server. Please help your child avoid saying names, where they live, or other private details.

When you tap “I understand — start,” Poppy will say the first question. The microphone stays off until you tap the corner microphone to answer; your browser may then ask for permission. Poppy listens for one short turn, then stops.
